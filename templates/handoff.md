# Research handoff — fillable record

- Work and scientific question: [scope]
- Scientific owner and other authority: [who may interpret, authorize, review, or execute]
- Accepted result and qualifications: [exact claim, decision date/locator]
- Current repository ID, branch, commit, and task-scope state: [fresh observations]
- Evidence and provenance: [inputs, source identities, commands, output, reviewer record]
- Superseded results or wording: [what was replaced and why]
- Residual mandate: [work still authorized, if any]
- Blockers and deferred findings: [observed stop or unevaluated area]
- Invalidation boundary: [what would defeat reuse]
- Checks not to repeat: [accepted checks while boundary holds]
- Next real decision boundary and owner: [action requiring a fresh decision]
- Recipient readback: [which artifacts were read and whether understanding was confirmed]

A handoff carries evidence for continuity. It does not grant permission, transfer scientific authority automatically, or prove comprehension.
