# Research handoff — fillable record

Use one repository when sufficient. Add an entry for each genuinely participating repository, including one that stays read-only.

- Purpose / decision question: [what the recipient needs to decide or continue]
- Scientific owner and other authority: [who may interpret, authorize, review, or execute in each repository]
- Repositories and exact identities: [repository ID/location, role in this task, branch, exact commit, observation date, relevant local changes; for a dependency, record provider and consumer separately and the selected provider revision]
- What is being handed off: [bounded claim, result, context, or stopped-state evidence]
- Evidence and provenance: [exact artifact locators and what each supports; inputs, source identities, methods/commands, observed results, review record; preserve material uncommitted diffs separately]
- Already reviewed/accepted and qualifications: [exact claim, responsible human, decision date/locator and claim ceiling; distinguish executor completion, technical validation, review and acceptance; say pending when unknown]
- Last observed state: [what was observed when, and what volatile facts the recipient must refresh before acting]
- Superseded results or wording: [what was replaced and why]
- Unresolved or pending work: [residual question, blockers, deferred findings and unevaluated areas; keep valid earlier results]
- Residual mandate: [work still authorized, if any; cite its separate authority rather than infer it from this record]
- Consumer checks still required, if applicable: [selected dependency, affected interface/configuration and downstream use; provider PASS cannot substitute for these checks]
- Invalidation boundary: [specific source, input, assumption, method, dependency or consumer-state changes, or missing/contradictory evidence that would defeat reuse; identify the affected claim]
- Checks not to repeat without invalidation: [accepted checks and evidence that remain valid; reopen only the invalidated or directly coupled delta]
- Permissions and authority not transferred: [this record grants no repository write permission, scientific authority, acceptance or authorization to rerun closed work; identify authorizations the recipient still needs]
- Next action and next human decision/acceptance boundary: [smallest continuation action, who must authorize it and who may accept the result separately in each repository]
- Recipient readback: [which artifacts were acquired/read and whether understanding was confirmed; record sending separately]

A handoff carries evidence for continuity. It does not prove compatibility, scientific correctness or comprehension. Separate repositories retain separate authority and acceptance; valid partial results do not imply a global transaction or automatic rollback.
