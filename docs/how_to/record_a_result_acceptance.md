# Record a result acceptance

Use this guide when the responsible researcher records acceptance of a bounded task or evidence layer, or the scientific lead decides whether reviewed evidence supports a precise scientific claim. These are separate decisions: the responsible Chat/lane can accept task evidence after review without granting scientific acceptance.

## Before you start

Have the decision question, exact result and source IDs, direct evidence, review findings, qualifications, and the decision maker's authority for the stated acceptance layer. See the [evidence states](../reference/evidence_and_task_states.md).

## Procedure

1. State the decision as `accepted`, `rejected`, or `pending` for one bounded claim. Example: “accepted: descriptive arithmetic for five synthetic measurements under independent Gaussian errors.”
2. Cite evidence path/hash/commit and the review basis. Record assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, and any blocking or nonblocking findings.
3. Name superseded report/note states and explain what remains valid. Record residual/open work and the invalidation boundary for reuse.
4. State the next authorized decision boundary and responsible researcher. Date or durably locate the decision; use the [handoff template](../../templates/handoff.md) when responsibility changes.

## Verify the result

Another researcher can distinguish the accepted claim from deferred real-data method, production, integration, publication, or licensing questions. The simulated teaching acceptance in [Tutorial 5](../tutorials/05_review_and_accept_returned_evidence.md) is labelled as such.

## Stop instead of proceeding if...

The reviewer evidence is unavailable, the decision maker lacks authority, or the claim is broader than the evidence. Executor completion and reviewer recommendation cannot be relabelled either task/evidence acceptance or scientific acceptance.
