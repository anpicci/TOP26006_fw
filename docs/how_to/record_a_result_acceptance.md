# Record a result acceptance

**Problem:** an owner decides whether reviewed evidence supports a specific claim.

Record the decision question, accepted result and scope, reviewer findings, evidence paths and identities, assumptions, uncertainty, and any qualifications. State explicitly which findings block broader claims and what remains deferred. Give the owner and decision date or durable locator. If acceptance is pending, say pending; an executor's success field must not be relabeled acceptance.

For the [synthetic lifecycle](../tutorials/05_review_and_accept_returned_evidence.md), the simulated owner role accepted only arithmetic under the stated model after provenance repair. Real-data method, publication, and future licensing were outside that teaching decision.
