# Record a result acceptance

Use this guide for two separate decisions: the research coordinator records bounded task/evidence acceptance after the required review for a stated scope; the scientific lead records scientific acceptance when reviewed evidence supports a precise scientific claim. Task/evidence acceptance neither authorizes new effects nor chooses a scientific method or interpretation. The research lead authorizes bounded tasks and effects within actual authority; this does not make that role either acceptor.

## Before you start

Have the decision question, exact result and source IDs, direct evidence, review findings, qualifications, and the decision maker's authority for the stated acceptance layer. See the [evidence states](../reference/evidence_and_task_states.md).

## Procedure

1. Name the acceptance layer and its decision maker: research coordinator for bounded task/evidence acceptance, scientific lead for scientific acceptance. State `accepted`, `rejected`, or `pending` for one bounded claim. Example of task/evidence acceptance: “accepted: the descriptive arithmetic record for five synthetic measurements under the stated independent Gaussian assumptions; no scientific model adoption.”
2. Cite evidence path/hash/commit and the review basis. Record assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, and any blocking or nonblocking findings.
3. Name superseded report/note states and explain what remains valid. Record residual/open work and the invalidation boundary for reuse.
4. State the next authorized decision boundary and responsible researcher. Date or durably locate the decision; use the [handoff template](../../templates/handoff.md) when responsibility changes.

## Verify the result

Another researcher can distinguish the accepted claim from deferred real-data method, production, integration, publication, or licensing questions. The simulated teaching acceptance in [Tutorial 5](../tutorials/05_review_and_accept_returned_evidence.md) is labelled as such.

## Stop instead of proceeding if...

The reviewer evidence is unavailable, the decision maker lacks authority, or the claim is broader than the evidence. Executor completion and reviewer recommendation cannot be relabelled either task/evidence acceptance or scientific acceptance.

## Generic example

The simulated research coordinator records bounded task/evidence acceptance of the five-row synthetic arithmetic record only, with the repaired note and review locator. Scientific acceptance remains a separate scientific-lead decision. One person may hold both roles, but the record names each responsibility and decision separately.

## What this does not establish

That record does not accept a real-data model, publication, or another repository's output.
