# Record a result acceptance

Use this guide when the human scientific owner decides whether reviewed evidence supports a precise result.

## Before you start

Have the decision question, exact result and source IDs, direct evidence, review findings, qualifications, and owner authority. See the [evidence states](../reference/evidence_and_task_states.md).

## Procedure

1. State the decision as `accepted`, `rejected`, or `pending` for one bounded claim. Example: “accepted: descriptive arithmetic for five synthetic measurements under independent Gaussian errors.”
2. Cite evidence path/hash/commit and the review basis. Record assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, and any blocking or nonblocking findings.
3. Name superseded report/note states and explain what remains valid. Record residual/open work and the invalidation boundary for reuse.
4. State the next authorized decision boundary and owner. Date or durably locate the decision; use the [handoff template](../../templates/handoff.md) when responsibility changes.

## Verify the result

Another researcher can distinguish the accepted claim from deferred real-data method, production, integration, publication, or licensing questions. The simulated teaching acceptance in [Tutorial 5](../tutorials/05_review_and_accept_returned_evidence.md) is labelled as such.

## Stop instead of proceeding if...

The reviewer evidence is unavailable, the decision maker lacks authority, or the claim is broader than the evidence. Executor completion and reviewer recommendation cannot be relabelled owner acceptance.

## Generic example

The human owner records acceptance of the five-row synthetic arithmetic only, with the repaired note and review locator.

## What this does not establish

That record does not accept a real-data model, publication, or another repository's output.
