# Scientific responsibility

This page explains responsibility for scientific method, interpretation and
scientific acceptance. Procedures and examples project this boundary; live
scientific/project authority determines who actually holds that remit.
See [decision layers](evidence_reproducibility_and_acceptance.md) for the
separate bounded task/evidence acceptance decision.

An AI executor can reproduce arithmetic, change a note, or report test output. Those acts cannot decide whether the uncertainty model represents a real experiment or whether the result merits a scientific claim. The person with actual scientific remit sets the question, method, acceptable evidence, and interpretation boundary. That may be a responsible researcher who also holds scientific-lead responsibility; the task title alone does not supply it. Independent review challenges the evidence before a separate scientific acceptance decision. One person may also authorize the task, but authorization and scientific acceptance remain distinct decisions.

The synthetic weighted mean assumes independent Gaussian errors and one common quantity. Its observed calculation supports a narrow arithmetic statement. Correlation, outliers, instrument provenance, and real-data applicability remain outside that statement. A methods repository may cite the qualified calculation without acquiring authority over the analysis source.

This distinction protects reproducibility and accountability. It also keeps execution permissions separate from scientific ownership. See [roles and authority](roles_and_authority.md) and [evidence and acceptance](evidence_reproducibility_and_acceptance.md).
