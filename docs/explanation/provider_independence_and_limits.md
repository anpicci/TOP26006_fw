# Why the research method can travel while provider controls differ

The stable sequence is a research question, a bounded task, authority and permissions, evidence, review, and human interpretation or acceptance. The [research workflow](research_workflow.md) and [roles and authority](roles_and_authority.md) explain these responsibilities before choosing a product. A provider UI change does not alter what evidence a scientific claim needs or who may accept it.

## Keep four layers distinct

**Research method** connects the question, assumptions, provenance and checks. **Project governance** determines who may authorize work, where it belongs and which execution/evidence rules apply. **Provider mechanisms** supply product surfaces, configuration, action controls, context and tools. **Local installation** supplies actual paths, versions, effective settings and observed behavior. These layers interact, but a capable tool or a provider permission prompt cannot create an owner grant.

The mechanism layer is replaceable, subject to a task's requirements and evidence. Another provider need not expose the same names or split discussion, research and repository execution into separate products. Map the needed responsibility to a documented mechanism, then identify the observation needed before relying on it. Similar names, instruction text or prompts do not establish equal loading, reach, sandboxing, review or restart behavior.

## Preserve decisions beyond product context

Keep the question, source/input revisions, actual actions/results and unresolved limits in a durable evidence record. Product context can help carry that material but cannot establish that an old file or summary matches current project state. On continuation, refresh only the material live facts and invalidated dependencies; a changed menu or mechanism name does not invalidate unrelated accepted research evidence.

Separate worker contexts can help divide work. They do not by themselves establish independence from the author, independent replication or acceptance. A hook can implement a mechanical action check without judging whether a statistical assumption fits the data. A connector or MCP path can make a service reachable without authorizing its use. Technical compatibility never establishes scientific compatibility. The [human evidence and acceptance boundary](evidence_reproducibility_and_acceptance.md) remains in force.

## Date mechanisms and check them when needed

Durable guidance should explain what to verify rather than preserve a roster of models, plan entitlements, quotas, prices or UI clicks. Such details can change independently of the research method. Keep dated official-source observations with the [provider compatibility status](../reference/provider_compatibility.md) and provider guides; recheck a mechanism when its current behavior matters to the next action. Official documentation establishes what the provider documents, not availability in a particular account, local compatibility, security certification, project authorization or scientific correctness.

The [OpenAI procedure](../how_to/providers/openai_reference_procedure.md) is the project's concrete reference example. The [Claude Code adaptation](../how_to/providers/anthropic_adaptation.md) is documentary and not locally qualified. Neither status ranks providers. Use the [adaptation guide](../how_to/adapt_to_another_ai_provider.md) to carry the method into a different surface under its own authorization; separately authorized local checks are needed for stronger support claims.

These are optional details beyond the beginner route. Return to the [manual](../index.md) or [prepare a research task](../how_to/ask_an_assistant_to_prepare_a_task.md) when the question needs only the common method.
