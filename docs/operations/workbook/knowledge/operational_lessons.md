# Operational lessons with evidence and conditions

Source IDs resolve in the [catalogue](../../source_catalog.md). These indications
are empirical lessons or native technical preconditions, not permissions.
Owner scientific choices are attributed in the cited records and do not become
general defaults.

## identity_and_scientific_applicability

Problem: a stable pathname or common bytes can hide a changed scientific use.
Condition: reusing a payload qualification or comparing results.
Evidence: `pkl_btv` binds input/sidecar/receipt identities;
`inference_r19` records distinct current/historical workspaces and dataset limits.
Indication: bind identity, semantic stage, selection, normalization, WC order,
blinding/dataset and actual consumer separately. Record inaccessible locators
as local limits. Counterexample: a same-byte expected-SM artifact does not
qualify observed inference; two independently normalized curves do not establish
raw-NLL nesting. A different payload at the same path cannot inherit a digest's
qualification. This is a technical/evidence precondition.

## source_and_final_are_not_disjoint

Problem: adding linked source and nonprompt final double-counts overlapping
processes. Condition: selecting BTV primaries plus alleged auxiliaries.
Evidence: `pkl_btv`'s native role/process-union checks; source products are enabled
and source/final populations overlap.
Indication: use the ten plan-selected finals; keep the sources as lineage.
Counterexample: a genuinely product-disabled, disjoint processor auxiliary may
be supported by the native mixed-stage contract after its own validation.
This is a native technical precondition, not a ban on all mixed-stage work.

## retain_valid_source_after_downstream_failure

Problem: reconstructing completed source work after a data-driven failure.
Condition: same native campaign/science, verified source receipt and exclusive
writer/collision checks. Evidence: TOPEFT
`docs/how_to/sr_reco_pkl_campaign.md` and `sr_extension`'s reconciled records.
Indication: use the native stage-aware recovery; inspect ambiguous outcome before
any authorized retry. Counterexample: changed source science or worker archive
digest can require a new campaign; source existence alone cannot authorize reuse.
This is a technical precondition, with campaign-specific empirical support.

## final_disposition_and_lifecycle_layers

Problem: an initial checkpoint or scheduler success is mistaken for final closure.
Condition: historical production or plot-state interpretation.
Evidence: `sr_extension`'s final closed_partial_by_owner supersedes its initial
in_progress; later Run3 recovery is narrower than successful production.
`tau5j` and `reference10`/072 separate persisted state, scheduler observation,
publication and selected content verification.
Indication: read the final scope-specific disposition and receipt; retain valid
technical evidence without inventing visual/scientific acceptance.
Counterexample: Run3 content verification is not an independent Run2 content
check; a newer UX commit does not repair a scientific membership contract.
This combines empirical guidance and native lifecycle preconditions.

## fitting_edges_do_not_select_fit_members

Problem: treating compatible bin edges as correct physical-category selection.
Condition: reference10 six-target fit-distribution consumption.
Evidence: `reference10`/071's six source-path fixture mismatches and held 070
command. Indication: withhold that dependent use until its fit-member contract
is resolved; retain the qualified mapping and other separable families.
Counterexample: a source fixture is not a measurement of actual PKL axes, and a
70-target count cannot prove each member's scientific contribution.
The hold is a cited owner/interface boundary, not an invented universal gate.

## keep_one_procedure_owner

Problem: copying commands into a workbook creates stale CLI bindings.
Condition: an existing how-to or skill already owns preparation, diagnostics
copying, evidence review or handoff. Evidence: maintained native manuals and
[playbook routes](../../playbook/index.md#reuse_framework_procedures).
Indication: link the owning entry point, check its current interface and prepare
only task-local bindings. Counterexample: a dated handoff has an existing output
or a retired option; do not execute it because it was once qualified. This is
maintenance guidance; a recipe or skill cannot select effects or science.

## calibrated_raw_and_display_q

Problem: transporting an ordinary display convention into calibrated inference,
or treating an old policy result as a new-start measurement.
Condition: consuming Sergio critical values or comparing successive calibrations.
Evidence: `sergio_calibration`'s maintained consumer repair and four-center records.
Indication: bind family, policy, attempted/finite/negative/selected/failure
populations and current successor artifact; calibrated comparisons use raw q.
Counterexample: finite rows in a failed worker can remain qualified through the
native collector; worker exit and scientific fit outcome are separate. An old
R6 calibration remains preserved but is not the R9 successor. Selection-conditioned
quantiles and uncertainty do not prove coverage. This is a native technical
contract backed by acquired production evidence, not new scientific acceptance.
