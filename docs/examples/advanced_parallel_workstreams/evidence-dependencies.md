# Evidence dependencies — synthetic teaching map

This map owns the dependency/validity projection. Acceptance metadata projects the routes in [workstreams](workstreams.md) and the corresponding events in [coordination](coordination.md); it must not independently redefine the actor, layer or scope of the same decision ID. `accepted` means the hypothetical decision for the exact revision, layer and scope below, not TOP26006_fw acceptance. ACC_CHECK_R1 at C02 is bounded task/evidence acceptance by the simulated research coordinator after REV_CHECK_R1, for raw-listing completeness only, with no scientific acceptance. Methods and explanation acceptances remain scientific-lead decisions; see [decision layers](../../explanation/evidence_reproducibility_and_acceptance.md). `stale_for_current_use` retains history but bars current downstream use. Execution, review, acceptance, and downstream readiness are separate fields. A dependency edge exists only through `consumed_input_ids`.
